      Computes the composite keyword score for a single term, from its TermFeatures via getTermFeatures.

      Formula:

      score = freqScore × difficulty × importance
      importance = 1 + Σ log2(flag)   over [meshWeight, patternBonus, nerBonus]
      • difficulty applies to every term, specialist or not. Previously, terms flagged as MeSH/NER/pattern/acronym skipped this signal entirely and fell back to a flat rarity-only bonus — which meant a MeSH term got credit for being domain-important but no credit for actually being hard to read.
      • importance combines the MeSH/pattern/NER flags additively in log-space rather than multiplying them directly, so a term hit by several flags at once grows gently instead of compounding into an outsized score.
      • acronymBonus is deliberately excluded from importance. eval/fitKeywordWeights.ts fit a linear model over all six TermFeatures (see that script's output) and its coefficient on log_acronymBonus came out ~0 and slightly negative — being an ALL-CAPS acronym wasn't actually predictive of importance once MeSH/pattern/NER were already in the mix, and the old flat ×2 bonus was over-promoting incidental acronyms. The feature is still computed (see getTermFeatures) so the eval script can keep tracking it, but it no longer feeds the production score.

      The other near-zero fitted coefficients (log_difficulty, log_meshWeight) are expected, not a signal to remove those terms — they only fire for specialist/domain terms, so across a general corpus their average marginal contribution is small even though they matter a lot on the documents where they do apply.

      These are still hand-picked weights — an implicit coefficient of 1 on every remaining term in the product, additive log-combination for importance — not the fitted linear regression itself. See eval/fitKeywordWeights.ts's header for how the two are compared before anything here gets hand-edited further.

      Word length is deliberately excluded — it over-promoted long but generic words above short meaningful ones (e.g. p53, WHO).

      Parameters

      • term: string

        Lowercase candidate term.

      • pageFreq: number

        Raw occurrence count on this page.

      • mesh: Map<string, number>

        MeSH term → weight map.

      • freq: Map<string, number>

        English frequency rank map.

      • scienceTerms: Set<string>

        Set of regex-detected science pattern terms.

      • nerTerms: Set<string>

        Set of compromise-extracted named entities.

      • acronyms: Set<string>

        Set of validated ALL-CAPS acronyms.

      Returns number

      Composite score (higher = more important keyword).
