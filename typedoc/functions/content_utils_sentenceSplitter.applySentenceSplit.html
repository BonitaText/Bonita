    Function applySentenceSplit

    • applySentenceSplit(opts?: ApplySentenceSplitOptions): Promise<void>

      Splits the densest thresholdPercent% of sentences across the page into bullets, leaving the rest of each paragraph as ordinary prose.

      Ranking is sentence-level and page-relative, not paragraph-level: every sentence in every eligible paragraph is scored independently and pooled into one ranking, so a single unusually dense sentence in an otherwise plain paragraph can be pulled out on its own, and a paragraph can end up with only some of its sentences bulleted rather than all-or-nothing.

      Candidate paragraphs still come from collectBlockElements with minWords: MIN_SPLIT_CANDIDATE_WORDS and the same structural exclusions as before (isCaptionOrCredit, isAlreadyPointForm, etc.) — that floor is about filtering out nav labels and captions before they're even considered, not about sentence count.

      Pipeline:

      1. Structural filter. Same as before — narrows elements down to real candidate paragraphs.
      2. Segment. For each candidate, build its normText/position map and find sentence spans via getSentenceSpans. Paragraphs with zero detected sentences are dropped here.
      3. Score. Every sentence across every candidate paragraph is scored individually via scoreParagraph (or takes its override action verbatim) and flattened into one page-wide list.
      4. Rank. selectSplitTargets ranks the whole flattened list by density and marks the top thresholdPercent%.
      5. Rebuild. Each paragraph with at least one selected sentence is rewritten via buildMixedContent; paragraphs with none stay untouched.

      Calls removeSentenceSplit at the start to ensure idempotency.

      Parameters

      • opts: ApplySentenceSplitOptions = {}

        See ApplySentenceSplitOptions.

      Returns Promise<void>
